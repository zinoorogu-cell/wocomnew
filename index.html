    One family.
    One assignment.

    A ministry born in Ibadan in 1985 with a mandate to fill the earth with the knowledge of God's glory, and to make sure nobody walks alone while we do it.

    Who we are

    Not a crowd. A family.

    Christ Family Assembly (CFA), the church arm of WOCOM, is a formidable congregation of God's sons and daughters. We are a family worshipping God, loving and caring for one another. Not only do we have a seat for you in the family, there is also a spot for you in the heart of our pastor, a figure of the love of the Father.

    No matter your race, tribe or background, a warm welcome and fellowship await you at CFA. We can't wait for your first visit. That's all it takes to make you part of this family of God.

    We are the church arm of Word Communication Ministries (WOCOM), founded in 1985 by Apostle Sunday Popoola and Rev (Mrs) Omowumi Popoola, with a mandate that reaches far beyond Ibadan: mass evangelistic crusades, television and radio broadcast, ministry training and children's outreach.

    IProviding Hope

    For the uncertain, the sinner, the discouraged, the frustrated and the shattered.

    IIProviding Home

    For the lonely, the neglected and all people, everybody belongs here.

    IIIProviding Health

    For those who are sick physically, emotionally and spiritually.

    IVProviding Help

    For the needy and the helpless, in practical and lasting ways.

    VProviding Harmony

    For families, and for those personally shattered by life.

    HomeLeadership

    Servants of the Word.

    Shepherds who have given their lives to one assignment: that you would know Christ, grow in Him, and finish your course with joy.

    President & Set Man · Word Communication Ministries

    Apostle Sunday A. Popoola

    Apostle Sunday Ayokunle Popoola is a seasoned minister of the Gospel with significant contributions to global evangelism and Christian leadership. He studied Chemistry at the University of Lagos, Nigeria, and holds advanced degrees in Religious Studies from Friends International Christian University (FICU), USA.

    With over forty-five years in the Pentecostal-Charismatic faith and full-time ministry, he has served in various ministry offices with deep insight into contemporary church movements. He is President and Founder of Word Communication Ministries and Senior Pastor of Christ Family Assembly, Ibadan.

    His ministry includes the "Bone of my Bone" marriage enrichment campaign, aimed at strengthening marriages globally. He also leads "The SCEPTRE," encouraging Christian cultural leadership, and chairs the Community Association for Leadership Motivation (CALM), promoting good governance and mentoring political leaders. He is International Director of the Apostolic Mentoring Network (AMEN).

    He is married to Omowumi Popoola, a lawyer and preacher, and together they continue to expand their ministry and influence.

    Senior Pastor

    Rev (Mrs) Veronica Omowumi Popoola

    Rev (Mrs) Veronica Omowumi Popoola is a senior pastor at Word Communication Ministries, a philanthropist and experienced missionary fundraising advocate with a unique combination of legal, pastoral and missionary engagement.

    A dedicated ministry leader with a strong commitment to the empowerment, mentorship and advancement of women across all walks of life, she brings over 35 years of experience in women's advocacy and pastoral care.

    She convenes two women-focused Christian events: The Unique Woman Conference, which helps women discover, develop and deploy their finest potential, and the National and International Women Intercessory Network, bringing women from different walks of life under one umbrella to bring positive change to society.

    With a deep understanding of Scripture and a collaborative approach to leadership, she equips women with the skills, confidence and opportunities needed to break barriers and reach their full potential. She is a much-loved conference speaker and a daily devotional writer, married to Apostle Sunday A. Popoola, together, favoured parents and grandparents.

    HomeThe Unique Woman

    Discover. Develop.
    Deploy.

    A conference and intercessory network for women who refuse to live beneath their design.

    The vision

    Finest potential, fully deployed.

    The Unique Woman Conference exists to help women discover, develop and deploy their finest potential in God, spiritually, professionally, in marriage, in motherhood and in leadership.

    Convened by Rev (Mrs) Omowumi Popoola, the conference gathers women from every walk of life under one roof for teaching, worship, prayer and honest conversation about the real life of a woman in today's world.

    Alongside the conference runs the National and International Women Intercessory Network, women standing together in prayer for their homes, churches and nations.

    Discover

    Your identity, gifting and the assignment God had in mind before you were born.

    Develop

    Skills, confidence and character, mentored by women who have gone ahead.

    Deploy

    Step into the room, the role and the responsibility you were built for.

    Join the network

    The Women Intercessory Network meets to pray for families, the church and the nations. If you carry a burden for prayer, this is your company.
